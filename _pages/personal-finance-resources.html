Textbook

  • Free Finance Book by Brent Evans
    This is the textbook I use to teach Personal Finance. It is an excellent, free resource that covers all of the key topics in personal finance. It should generally be the first place you look for information, before turning to sources more specific to your situation or topic.

Retirement

  • Retirement Calculator (Ramsey Solutions)
    This is the retirement calculator I use most because it is simple and easy to use. There is nothing special about this one in particular; many similar calculators are available online.

Credit Cards

  • Best Credit Cards (NerdWallet)
    An up-to-date summary of the best credit cards currently available, with a description of what each card is best suited for.
  • Travel Credit Cards by Brent Evans
    A more in-depth look at the best travel credit cards from the author of the Free Finance Book.

Taxes

  • Federal Tax Tables (Free Finance Book)
    The current federal income tax tables, kept up to date.

Podcasts

  • Miles to Go
    A short weekly podcast covering the latest travel hacks, updates to travel credit cards, and other travel news.
  • ChooseFI
    A podcast about achieving financial independence. Early episodes focus on the hosts' own path to financial independence and general personal finance advice. Recent episodes are mostly guest interviews on specific topics such as student loans, paying for college, and planning for retirement.
